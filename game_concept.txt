THE DESCENT PROTOCOL – Game Design Document
Overview
THE DESCENT PROTOCOL is a top-down sci-fi shooter that throws players into a 30-floor descent into an alien-infested facility. The gameplay is fast, brutal, and swarm-heavy. With escalating threats, upgrade phases, mid-floor sabotage puzzles, and evolving enemy types, this game balances tension, gore, and strategic action.
Core Gameplay Loop
1. Spawn on new floor
2. Complete critical objective (e.g., restore power, unlock doors)
3. Survive escalating swarms of enemies
4. Clear all threats on the floor
5. Enter upgrade chamber
6. Choose one of three randomized upgrades
7. Descend automatically to next floor
Weapons and Drone System
Players begin with basic gear and expand their arsenal through upgrades.
Weapon Types:
- Kinetic Rifle
- Pulse Shotgun
- Flamethrower
- Grenade Launcher
- Tesla Arc Cannon
- Needle Railgun
- Bio-Harvester
Weapon Mods:
- Explosive Tips
- Cryo Chamber
- Vampiric Coil
- EMP Converter
Drone Variants:
- Aegis (Default)
- Torchlet
- Needler
- Bastion
- Harvestor
- Specter
Level Progression and Floor Tasks
Each floor presents a unique objective or hazard before allowing the player to proceed.
1. Breaching – Restore power systems
2. The Armory – Unlock heavy weapon lockers
3. Gravity Well – Disable magnetic locks
4. Dark Sector – Navigate in darkness
5. Nesting Grounds – Burn alien biomass
6. Security Network – Hack reactor terminals
7. Ventilation – Redirect airflow safely
8. Biomass Overgrowth – Purge infestation
9. System Purge – Reboot core systems
10. Cocooned Outpost – Find keycards under swarm pressure
11. Fusion Complex – Stabilize the central reactor
12. Egg Chamber – Destroy glowing sacs before hatching
13. Lockdown – Neutralize elite Swarmer Prime
14. Swarm Nest – Survive endless waves until objective clears
15. Cryo Vault – Defrost frozen systems while under siege
