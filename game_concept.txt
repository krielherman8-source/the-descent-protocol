THE DESCENT PROTOCOL – Game Design Document
Overview
THE DESCENT PROTOCOL is a top-down sci-fi shooter that throws players into a 30-floor descent into an alien-infested facility. The gameplay is fast, brutal, and swarm-heavy. With escalating threats, upgrade phases, mid-floor sabotage puzzles, and evolving enemy types, this game balances tension, gore, and strategic action.
Core Gameplay Loop
1. Spawn on new floor
2. Complete critical objective (e.g., restore power, unlock doors)
3. Survive escalating swarms of enemies
4. Clear all threats on the floor
5. Enter upgrade chamber
6. Choose one of three randomized upgrades
7. Descend automatically to next floor
Weapons and Drone System

Level Progression and Floor Tasks
Each floor has a reason (why the job matters), a concrete goal, and a line shown when the job is done. The briefing card at floor start shows the name, the reason and the goal. It fades, or dismisses on a tap or key, without pausing the fight. The HUD repeats the goal with a progress count. Endless mode reuses these same reasons on rebuilt decks. An endless boss uses the boss reason below, not the keycard errand (the Warden's Return is a boss fight).

Floor | Name | Goal | Why | When it is done
1 | Breaching | Restore power to the lift | The lift is dead. Restart the generators to power it and descend. | Lift powered. Clear a path, then take the exit.
2 | The Armory | Search the lockers | Security lockers hold a weapon and the key for the next lift. Search them. | Locker opened. The way down is unlocked.
3 | Gravity Well | Disable the mag-locks | Mag-locks are pinning the lift. Cut them, and stay out of the gravity well. | Locks released. The lift can drop. Leave the well.
4 | Dark Sector | Light the beacon relay | The lights are dead. Relay the beacons in order so the next shaft can see you. | Relay lit. The dark sector is mapped. Head for the exit.
5 | Nesting Grounds | Burn the biomass | Biomass is clogging the descent shaft. Burn it out or the lift cannot pass. | Shaft cleared. The biomass is ash. Reach the exit.
6 | Security Network | Hack the reactor terminals | Reactor terminals still hold the security bypass. Hack them before the floor seals. | Bypass accepted. Security is open. Move to the exit.
7 | Ventilation | Seal the vent valves | The air currents blow you off the catwalks. Seal each valve to stop one current. | Airflow stopped. The catwalks are safe. Descend.
8 | Biomass Overgrowth | Purge the spore nodes | Spore muck is eating the deck. Purge the nodes or the floor collapses under you. | Spores burned out. The deck will hold. Find the exit.
9 | System Purge | Reboot every core node | The core nodes must come up together, or the reboot aborts and the lift stays dark. | Systems back online. The floor accepts the descent.
10 | Cocooned Outpost | Recover the keycards | Three keycards are sealed in cocoons. They open the security door, and whatever waits behind it. | The warden is down. The outpost door is yours.
11 | Fusion Complex | Stabilize the reactor | The central reactor is climbing. Hold the core ring until it stabilizes, or the shaft melts. | Reactor stable. The heat drops. Take the exit.
12 | Egg Chamber | Destroy the egg sacs | These sacs hatch swarmers. Destroy them before they break open. | Brood destroyed. Nothing left to hatch. Move on.
13 | Lockdown | Kill the Swarmer Prime | An elite Swarmer Prime has sealed this deck. Kill it or the lockdown never lifts. | Prime is down. Lockdown released. Reach the exit.
14 | Swarm Nest | Survive the nest | The nest will not open the next hatch until you outlast the swarm. | The swarm breaks. The hatch unlocks. Go.
15 | Cryo Vault | Defrost the vault systems | Frozen systems are locking the vault doors. Thaw them, and stay out of the frost. | Vault thawed. The doors cycle open. Descend.
16 | Pumpworks | Restart the flood pumps | The lower decks are flooding. Restart the pumps or the lift drowns. | Pumps online. The water drops. Head down.
17 | Relay Spire | Escort the relay to the spire | The relay core has to reach the spire, or the floors below stay deaf to your signal. | Relay seated. The spire is transmitting. Move out.
18 | Quarantine Labs | Deliver the power cells | The containment grid is dark. Carry the power cells to the socket before the labs spill. | Grid powered. Containment holds. Leave the labs.
19 | Data Core | Reboot the data core | The data core only boots if every node is live before the timer dies. Do it in the dark. | Core online. The archive answers. Continue down.
20 | Hive Threshold | Slay the Hive Colossus | The Colossus is the hive's gate. It has to fall before the tunnels let you through. | The gate-beast is dead. The hive opens. Go deeper.
21 | Brood Tunnels | Burn the brood sacs | These tunnels breed the next wave. Burn the sacs before they hatch behind you. | Tunnels are quiet. The brood is ash. Press on.
22 | Spore Fields | Purge the spore field | The spore field is seeding every deck above. Purge the nodes so the infection cannot climb. | Field sterilized. The spores stop rising. Descend.
23 | Neural Lattice | Relay the neural beacons | The lattice is the Overmind's nerves. Relay the beacons in order to blind the floors below. | Lattice blinded. It cannot see the next shaft. Move.
24 | Acid Cisterns | Seal the acid valves | Acid vents are eating the cistern lifts. Seal each valve to stop one flood current. | Vents sealed. The acid settles. Take the lift.
25 | Brood Throne | Kill the Hive Matriarch | The Matriarch lays every sac you have burned. Kill her or the hive replaces itself. | The mother is dead. The laying stops. Go to the core.
26 | Core Breach | Defend the coolant core | Sappers are cracking the last coolant core. Hold them off until the seal finishes. | Core sealed. Coolant holds. The breach is shut.
27 | Gravity Collapse | Disable the collapse locks | The collapse locks are all that keep this deck from crushing the shaft. | Locks open. The deck holds long enough. Get to the exit.
28 | Dead Sector | Hold out in the dark | Something hunts in the dead dark. Live through it and the core airlock will cycle. | You outlasted it. The airlock cycles. Go.
29 | The Heart | Burn out the hive heart | The hive heart feeds the Overmind. Burn it out or the last floor never weakens. | The heart is cinders. The Overmind is alone. Descend.
30 | The Overmind | Destroy the Overmind | This is the mind that took Kestrel-9. Destroy it and the swarm above you dies with it. | The Overmind is silent. Reach extraction.

Endless boss — Warden's Return | Kill the Brood Warden | The Brood Warden is the hive's first gate. It will not let the shaft open while it lives. | Warden down. This gate is open. Go deeper.
Endless bosses Prime, Colossus, Matriarch and Overmind use the same reason as floors 13, 20, 25 and 30.
